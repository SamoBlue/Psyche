# Normal Levels

🏜️ Egypt · Noon Safe
▼
Starting Point: Inside a Bedouin tent during a sandstorm. AIRIS cannot accompany you — her systems would be damaged.
Inside the tent · Sandstorm rages
After the storm · Pyramids revealed

🏜️ Exploration

Venture into the sandstorm. Limited visibility. Find artifacts half-buried — pottery, hieroglyphs, a weathered map.

🌊 The Reveal

The storm settles. The Nile stretches before you with a bridge. In the distance — the pyramids.

🎯 The Guess

Cross the bridge. AIRIS waits. Clues point to Egypt. One guess. Make it count.

🎯 Clues:
  • Nile River
  • Pyramids of Giza
  • Desert landscape
  • Hieroglyphic fragments
🗼 Paris · Sunset Creepy
▶
Starting Point: You arrive on a Parisian boulevard at sunset. Thick fog rolls through the streets. AIRIS is nowhere to be found.
Foggy boulevard · Empty streets
Eiffel Tower · Obscured by fog

🌫️ Alone in the Fog

Empty streets. Mannequins in shop windows seem to watch you. Flickering street lights. Hollow footsteps.

🚗 Unsettling Details

Abandoned Citroën cars. A newspaper kiosk with headlines about "The Disappearance." Shadows move when you're not looking.

⏱️ The Search

After 10 minutes, you find her. Standing outside a café, perfectly still, as if waiting. She doesn't acknowledge you until you're close.

👁️ Disturbing Detail: When you first find AIRIS, she's completely motionless. No idle animation. No blinking. For exactly 3 seconds after you approach, she doesn't move at all. Then she "activates."
🎯 Clues:
  • Eiffel Tower visible through fog
  • French language on signs
  • Haussmann architecture
  • Baguettes in abandoned bakeries
