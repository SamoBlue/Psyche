IAMAI // GEORUST CHRONICLES

Where global exploration meets psychological fracture — a GeoGuessr x Rusty Lake descent.

⚡ IAMAI (codename GeoBot) — a fusion of GeoGuessr and mind-bending horror (MiSide, DDLC, SOMA). You are paired with AI-RIS, a cryptic robot girl. Your mission: explore abandoned lands, scan corrupted objects, and identify the correct country from environmental clues. But after 3 normal levels, reality warps. A hospital with 3 floors awaits — where the machine wears a smile.
