THE 12 CELL GAME

A psychological death-game mystery where twelve students are trapped
inside a prison simulation — but the exit is a lie.

20 Rounds
12 Students
1 Survivor
0 Escape

⎯ THE MYSTERY ⎯

The students believe they leave the simulation after each round.
But the resting room they wake up in is also fake.

The entire world around them is layered simulations created by
Yuni Miyafumi. Every memory, every death, every scream — manufactured.

Hita Tane plans to win the game, kill Yuni,
and trap everyone inside forever. No one will ever log out.

⎯ THE 12 STUDENTS ⎯

Rakukite Rina

The Hero

Calm and empathetic leader trying to stop the game. But can she save anyone?

Yanashi Kanedori

The Loyal Friend

Friendly and comforting, but terrified of abandonment. Her loyalty hides cracks.

Hagimida Marize

The Observer

A mystery novelist obsessed with uncovering truth. But some truths consume.

Hita Tane

The Monster

A manipulative philosopher fascinated by corruption. He volunteered for this.

Yuni Miyafumi

The Scientist

The creator of the simulation and only escape route. But is she a savior or jailer?

Kureha Shinomi

The Idol

A famous performer hiding severe anxiety. Her mask is bleeding through.

Daichi Reizan

The Fighter

A boxer slowly consumed by anger. His fists remember what his mind forgets.

Mikoto Fuyume

The Liar

An actress who constantly changes personalities. Even she doesn't know the real self.

Ren Amagiri

The Hacker

A programmer discovering hidden code in the prison. He sees the strings.

Sora Ichinose

The Believer

A spiritual girl hearing mysterious voices. They whisper things beyond logic.

Akuro Vance

The Strategist

A cold chess champion treating life like a game. He will sacrifice anyone.

Noa Kisaragi

The Child

A shy memory prodigy hiding a terrifying secret. She remembers every death.
